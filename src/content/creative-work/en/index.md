title: Creative Work
---

Creative writing has been an important part of who I am from an early age. I am grateful that some of my work has already found its way into the world independently, and I hope it continues to serve meaningful purposes.

**2009** – Award for outstanding journalism in a national philology competition.

**Since 2009** – I have regularly contributed to the Lithuanian press, initially writing on a variety of topics and later focusing primarily on psychological issues.

**2013–2015** – Together with composer Kęstutis Daugirdas, I created _[Singing the Revolution](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=15lKhXhKljc)_, a musical work about Lithuanian history. The piece was performed at the Lithuanian Song Festival in Chicago in 2015.

**Since 2019** – I have been involved in the _Childhood Without Violence_ project run by the Lithuanian news portal _lrytas.lt_. I regularly write articles for the portal on parenting and child development.

**2019** – Together with artist Vytautas Tautkevičius, I published the children's book _The Adventures of Jonas the Little Dragon: How to Defeat Dragonitis_. This playful and popular book helps children become familiar with hospitals and medical procedures, reducing anxiety associated with medical care.

**Since 2020** – I have been working with Oak9 Entertainment on the _Stomp! Stomp! Rhinos!_ project. We are creating an animated series and a series of books for preschool children about emotions and emotional regulation.

You can learn more about the project here: [Licence Global Fast Chat. Presenting the Stomp! Stomp! Rhinos! Series. Why emotional regulation matters.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hNvkZr2Jp6g&t=252s)
